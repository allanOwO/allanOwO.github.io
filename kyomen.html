Kyomen

Don't let the weight of your past collapse the world. Kyomen is a fast-paced isometric arena combat game built around dynamic mask-based gameplay.

Role: Programmer | Team project | Global Game Jam 2026

KYOMEN

My Role

I worked on the combat programming that drives the game's encounters. My systems were responsible for creating escalating pressure, controlling how enemies approached the player, and giving the boss enough reactive behaviour to make fights feel less predictable.

Technical Contributions

  • Designed and implemented the enemy wave system to control encounter pacing.
  • Created enemy movement and attack behaviours, coordinating navigation and combat states.
  • Implemented boss attack patterns and reactive decision-making to respond to the player's actions.

What I Learned

This project strengthened my experience designing gameplay systems under a tight game-jam deadline and balancing reusable enemy logic with the specific needs of a boss encounter.
