Just a Chill Fishing Game

Explore the deepest depths, discover fish, and make Marty proud in this relaxed fishing experience created for Global Game Jam 2025.

Role: Gameplay Programmer | 48-hour game jam | Global Game Jam 2025

My Role

I helped take the game from concept to a complete playable release within the 48-hour jam. My focus was on the interaction that defines the experience: fishing, discovering life underwater, and making the environment feel active.

Technical Contributions

  • Designed and implemented the core fishing mechanic, from player interaction through to the catch feedback.
  • Implemented fish behaviours to make the underwater exploration feel rewarding.
  • Created bubble behaviours and environmental feedback to add movement and atmosphere.
  • Worked within the jam deadline to help deliver a complete and playable project.

What I Learned

This project strengthened my ability to scope features, iterate quickly, and deliver polished gameplay under a strict time limit.
